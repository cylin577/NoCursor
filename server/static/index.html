Help train a human cursor model

NoCursor studies how humans move a mouse. Your task: click targets that appear on screen, then move naturally for a few seconds. Takes about 1–2 minutes.

What we collect: anonymous cursor positions, timestamps and clicks (mouse only) during the tasks.
What we never collect: your identity, IP address, keystrokes, screen contents or anything typed on this page. Data is stored anonymously and used only to train movement models.
